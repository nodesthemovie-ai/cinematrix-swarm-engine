## 💡 Pro Tips for Success

### For Social Media Posts
✅ Post during business hours (9am-2pm your timezone)
✅ Space posts 1 day apart for maximum reach
✅ Respond to EVERY comment/question within 24 hours
✅ Share user-generated content (if you get any)
✅ Pin your best performing post

### For Devpost
✅ Include a screenshot or demo video
✅ Keep description 3-5 paragraphs (not too long)
✅ Link to your GitHub repo prominently
✅ Monitor daily for comments first week
✅ Fix any reported issues immediately

### For GitHub Discussions
✅ Post when GitHub is actively monitored (Tuesday-Thursday)
✅ Reply to every discussion comment
✅ Add follow-up comments to prompt engagement
✅ Update as new features/developments happen

### For Film School Outreach
✅ Personalize each email (not mass copy-paste)
✅ Focus on how CineMatrix helps THEIR students
✅ Keep emails to 150-200 words (quick read)
✅ Follow up after 7 days if no response
✅ Track responses in spreadsheet

### For Dialogue Testing
✅ Test with 2-3 different premises (vary genre/scope)
✅ Score honestly (this data shapes v1.2.0)
✅ Save all test outputs as examples
✅ Note specific quoted dialogue that works/fails
✅ Update v1.2.0 roadmap based on findings

## ❓ FAQ for Execution

**Q: Do I need to post all content myself?**  
A: Yes! Personal engagement drives authenticity and community trust. But you can schedule posts in advance using Buffer/Hootsuite.

**Q: Can I skip any of the 9 steps?**  
A: You can prioritize, but all 9 create different engagement/feedback loops. Devpost + GitHub Discussions are highest priority. Dialogue testing informs v1.2.0.

**Q: What if I get negative feedback?**  
A: Use the criticism response template in ENGAGEMENT_RESPONSE_LIBRARY.md. Embrace it — negative feedback is valuable.

**Q: How long will 9 steps take total?**  
A: 2-3 weeks at 1-2 hours per day. You can compress to 1 week by doing more daily. Spread over month is ideal for sustained engagement.

**Q: What should I do after completing all 9?**  
A: Merge feature/dialogue-generation to develop branch and prepare v1.2.0 release. Use collected feedback to refine dialogue generation output.
