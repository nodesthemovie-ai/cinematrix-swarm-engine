## What is CineMatrix?

CineMatrix coordinates four autonomous creative agents to analyze film concepts 
from multiple perspectives:

👔 **Executive Producer** — Market positioning, competitive analysis, greenlight decisions  
✍️ **Screenwriter** — Narrative structure, character psychology, 3-act breakdown  
🎥 **Cinematographer** — Visual language, production design, technical requirements  
💰 **Financial Analyst** — Budget estimates, ROI projections, financing pathways  

**The Result:** A comprehensive "Sovereign Dossier" containing everything a 
production team needs to greenlight a film.

---

## Why CineMatrix?

**For Filmmakers:**
- Get professional-grade analysis of your concepts in minutes
- Understand what producers, screenwriters, and cinematographers think about your idea
- Identify gaps in story, feasibility, and market positioning before pitching

**For Educators:**
- Teach production reality (all perspectives, not just story)
- Show how different roles think about the same problem
- Give students instant feedback on their ideas

**For Developers:**
- Explore multi-agent AI coordination
- See how to prevent AI hallucinations with zero-trust architecture
- Build on an open-source, production-ready foundation

---

## Key Features

✅ **LLM-Agnostic** — Works with Claude, GPT-4, Google AI, or any LLM  
✅ **Zero-Trust Architecture** — Prevents recursive paradoxes & hallucinations  
✅ **Production-Ready** — Complete dossiers in 2-5 minutes  
✅ **No Setup Required** — Copy prompt, use any LLM, get results
